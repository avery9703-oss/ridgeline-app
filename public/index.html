How It Works

01
Book online

Pick a car and your dates. Price is shown up front, no surprise fees at pickup.

02
Get verified

Upload your license, take a quick selfie, and consent to a driving record check.

03
Sign & pay

E-sign the rental agreement, then place a refundable deposit hold plus the rental charge.

04
Pick up

Meet at the car or use keyless entry. Return it the way you found it.

Pricing & Policies

Renters must be 21+ with a valid license held 2+ years. A refundable deposit (held, not charged) is placed at booking and released within 3 business days of return. 150 free miles/day, $0.45/mile after. Full tank to full tank. Cancellations 48+ hours out are fully refunded.
